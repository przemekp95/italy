## Decisions and reconciliations

- **Income thresholds are not RAL thresholds.** National and local income tests use taxable employment income after mandatory employee INPS. Tests therefore exercise policy functions directly at taxable thresholds and separately verify the RAL-to-taxable transformation.
- **Milan has a legal cliff.** At €23,000.00 taxable income municipal tax is zero; at €23,000.01 it is 0.8% of the entire income. Global net monotonicity is therefore not asserted.
- **The MEF portal has no 2026 Milan row yet.** The municipal regulation keeps the prior rate in force until changed, and the official 2026–2028 budget says the 0.8% rate and €23,000 exemption are unchanged.
- **No silent massimale cap.** Applicability depends on first pension enrollment / valid contributory-system election, facts the one-input product does not collect. The estimate continues the simplified contribution formula and warns at the boundary.
- **Annual liability, not withholding simulation.** Addizionali may appear as prior-year balance/current-year advance across real payslips. “Average per pay period” is annual estimated net divided by 12, 13 or 14.
